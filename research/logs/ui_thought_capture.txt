Calculate clock hand angle
A clock shows 3:15. What is the angle between the hour and minute hands? Think through the calculation step by step.
5:51 PM
At 3:15:
1) Minute hand angle from 12
Each minute is (6^circ) (because (360^circ/60=6^circ)).
At 15 minutes: (15 times 6^circ = 90^circ).
2) Hour hand angle from 12
Each hour mark is (30^circ) (because (360^circ/12=30^circ)).
At 3:00, the hour hand is at (3 times 30^circ = 90^circ).
The hour hand also moves as minutes pass: it moves (30^circ) per hour (=frac{30^circ}{60}=0.5^circ) per minute.
In 15 minutes, it moves (15 times 0.5^circ = 7.5^circ).
So at 3:15, the hour hand is at (90^circ + 7.5^circ = 97.5^circ).
3) Angle between them
Difference: (|97.5^circ - 90^circ| = 7.5^circ).
Answer: (7.5^circ) (the smaller angle).
